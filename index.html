          How to play
          1. Press on your sharpener — the one with the dashed ring.
          2. Drag away from where you want it to go, like a slingshot. Pull further for more power.
          3. Let go. The dotted line shows where it starts off.
          4. Push theirs over the edge to win the round. Fall off yourself and you lose it.

          Score is chalked on the board. Best of five.
